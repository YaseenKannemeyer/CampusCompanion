Campus Companion Help

Welcome to the Campus Companion Help Center. Here you can find guidance on how to use the application effectively and troubleshoot common issues.

Getting Started

  • Login with your student credentials to access your account.
  • Navigate through your timetable, notifications, and personal profile.
  • Update your account details via the Settings section.

Using the Application

  • View your upcoming classes and assignments.
  • Check notifications for important announcements.
  • Access resources and documents shared by your lecturers.

Password Recovery

  • If you forget your password, contact us using the Contact page.
  • Our support team will reset your account and provide temporary access.
  • Once logged in, you can change your password in the Settings section of the application.

Contact Support

For further assistance, email us at support@campuscompanion.com or use the Contact page in the application to submit a support request.
